Rwanda Context

This is an academic simulation inspired by HIV prevention and treatment challenges in Rwanda. It is designed to explore intervention behaviour, not to replace clinical or policy data.

Model Story

The dashboard turns the SITA equations into an interactive tool: S moves to I through transmission, I can move to T through testing and treatment-seeking, and disease progression is tracked through A.

Special Part

The fractional order q lets the model remember past states. During the presentation, compare q=1 with q<1 to show why fractional memory matters.

Defense Message

The strongest evidence is the dashboard itself: it connects theory, computation, intervention scenarios, R0, sensitivity, and chapter-ready results in one working system.
